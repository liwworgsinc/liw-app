Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information LIW Worgs Inc. collects through this website and client portal, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, company name, contact preferences, account credentials, service requests, appointment details, messages, uploaded documents, photographs, and payment or invoice-related information.

Depending on the service you request, submitted materials may contain financial, property, tax, credit, identification, optical, or other sensitive information. Please upload sensitive documents only when they are necessary for your service and through the secure portal provided.

We may also collect basic technical data such as browser type, device information, pages viewed, timestamps, approximate location derived from an internet address, authentication events, and security logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and protect accounts, respond to inquiries, evaluate and perform requested services, collect documents, schedule appointments, prepare estimates and invoices, process or record payments, provide customer support, communicate status updates, improve the platform, prevent fraud, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

We may share information with employees and authorized contractors who need it to perform their work; technology, hosting, storage, email, payment, analytics, and security providers; licensed professionals and service partners involved in your request; and government authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and platform security.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not allow advertisers to access your private portal documents.

4. Supabase and other service providers

The client portal uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and secure file storage. Other providers may be used for hosting, payments, email delivery, scheduling, communications, or analytics. These providers process information under their own privacy and security terms and only for the functions they provide to us.

5. Cookies and local storage

The portal may use cookies or browser storage to keep you signed in, remember session information, protect account access, and support essential site functions. Blocking these technologies may prevent parts of the portal from working properly.

6. Data security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including authenticated accounts, database access controls, private storage rules, and role-based permissions. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so you should use a strong unique password and avoid sending sensitive documents through ordinary email unless instructed.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect the platform, and satisfy legal or regulatory requirements. Retention periods may vary based on the service and type of record.

8. Your choices and requests

You may update certain profile information through the client portal. You may also ask to review, correct, or delete personal information, subject to identity verification and legal or recordkeeping requirements. We may retain information that must be kept for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, contractual, professional, or legal reasons.

To submit a privacy request, email liwworgsinc@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line.

9. Children's privacy

The website and portal are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create portal accounts for children under 13 without appropriate authorization. Contact us if you believe a child provided information improperly.

10. External links

Our site may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies, and LIW Worgs Inc. is not responsible for those external practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
