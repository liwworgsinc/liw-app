# LIW Tailored Service Forms V6

The LIW intake is now service-specific. Each service record in Supabase includes:

- A service-specific form heading and introduction
- A tailored request-title example
- A sensitive-information warning
- A document preparation checklist
- Detailed intake fields organized into sections
- Conditional questions that appear only when relevant

## Form coverage

- Real Estate: buyer, seller, residential rental, and commercial lease paths
- Property Management: property profile, occupancy, management services, challenges, and urgent issues
- Tax Preparation: return type, tax years, filing profile, income sources, business questions, and notices
- Credit Solutions: goals, reports, item types, prior disputes, identity-theft concerns, and acknowledgements
- Business Funding: business profile, funding amount, use of funds, revenue, debt, and statement readiness
- Business Advertising: deliverables, campaign goals, audience, creative assets, dates, and budget
- Web Design: project type, goals, pages, features, integrations, content, timeline, and support
- Eyeglasses Repair: frame identification, construction, damage, prior repair, urgency, and service method
- Digital Business Cards: public profile information, actions, links, NFC options, and design direction

Sensitive credentials and identity numbers should never be collected in the standard intake form. Requested records are uploaded after submission through the secure client portal.
